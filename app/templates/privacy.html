Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Cellusys CodeCamp (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains the personal information handled when you visit this website, contact us, subscribe to updates, or apply to a CodeCamp programme.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

  • Account details such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Application details such as date of birth, age, gender, location, education or professional background, programme preferences and attendance availability.
  • Recruitment information such as assessment answers and scores, interview bookings, application status, cohort assignment and related messages.
  • Information you include in a contact message or provide when requesting website updates.
  • Technical and security information generated when you use the website, such as session and access logs.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Manage accounts and respond to requests.
  • Review applications and administer assessments and interviews.
  • Communicate application decisions, programme updates and reminders.
  • Manage cohorts, maintain the service and protect it from misuse.

Service providers and sharing

Access to applicant information is limited to people who need it to administer the programme. We may use hosting, database, email delivery, monitoring and other service providers to operate the website. Those providers process information on our behalf as applicable to the services enabled for this deployment. The hosted configuration uses Render for application hosting and media storage, a PostgreSQL database (which may be hosted by Supabase), Resend for email, and Redis for sessions and service operations. Sentry monitoring and Google Sheets synchronization may also be enabled. These providers may process information in countries other than where you live. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to manage the application process, provide the service, resolve disputes and meet applicable obligations. Retention periods can vary by record and operational requirements. We use access controls and other safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to stop receiving non-essential updates. We will consider requests in accordance with applicable law and may need to retain some records for legitimate operational or legal reasons.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@cellusyscodecamp.com.
